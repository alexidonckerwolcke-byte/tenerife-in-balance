The challenge

When tourism grows too fast, island life feels the pressure.

Tenerife is one of Spain's Canary Islands, in the Atlantic Ocean. It is an island with local communities and natural landscapes—not just one resort or city.

As shown in our presentation, around 5.8 million visitors come each year, alongside about 900,000 residents. Tourism supports many livelihoods, but unmanaged growth can add pressure to housing, roads, water and natural places.

This campaign is not against visitors. It asks decision-makers, businesses and travellers to help tourism respect the people and place that make Tenerife special.
