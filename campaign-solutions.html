The goal is not to reject visitors. It is to manage tourism so that local people, island nature and visitors can all thrive.

What needs to change

Six steps toward a better balance.

These are campaign priorities: practical areas where public policy, businesses and visitors can make a difference.

01

Keep homes for residents

Support housing policies that make it easier for local people to live in their communities.

02

Manage visitor pressure

Monitor crowding and manage visitor numbers where places and services are under strain.

03

Protect nature and water

Safeguard coastlines and habitats, and use the island's water responsibly.

04

Make getting around easier

Improve public transport and walking routes so fewer journeys depend on cars.

05

Spread visits through the year

Encourage travel beyond the busiest times and places, where communities support it.

06

Give residents a real voice

Include local communities in tourism decisions, with clear rules and progress updates.

The principle

Listen to residents.
Plan for the long term.
Protect what makes the island special.
